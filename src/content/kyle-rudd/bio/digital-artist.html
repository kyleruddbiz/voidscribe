I bring an artist’s eye to just about everything I work on.

For digital art specifically, I started with imitating flash animations by misusing PowerPoint. It was keyframe animation done by copying every vector shape on the slide and editing between each frame. Not very efficient, but I made some fun (sadly lost) videos with it.

I moved on to Photoshop Elements, then various versions of Photoshop, until Adobe started abusing their customers too much. These days I use Affinity Photo for most of my work.

Starting a new piece taps me right into the flow state. Many projects like album artwork or shirts have a purpose, but often I’ll just play around and enjoy the sense of presence.
