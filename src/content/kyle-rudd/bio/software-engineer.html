I have been a full-stack software engineer for 13+ years, working across web and desktop, and more recently game development. I specialize in C# and .NET, with TypeScript, Unity, and more mixed in.

Coding is pure creation. It feels incredible to build something from nothing with fine control over every detail. I respect the craft deeply.

And then there’s AI. I’ve never had such aggressively conflicting feelings about a technology. Let’s sidestep the abyss of negatives for now. Can the craft survive AI? I think so. If we’re intentional about design, validation, and review. If we’re willing to reject default outputs and ask questions. If we’re willing to do what it takes to maintain authorship. Then I believe the craft will survive. And who knows, maybe even thrive.

AI will never replace a team of quality people, though. Nor should it. There’s nothing quite like working with brilliant people towards a terrifyingly ambitious goal. This is what I strive for.
